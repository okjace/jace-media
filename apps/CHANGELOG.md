# Jace Media apps

Changes to the apps themselves. Pages that open the site inside the apps (and the TV app) update with the
site, without an app update.

## 2.2.0

- New button in the middle of the tab bar on phones: write a post, upload a video, or go live.
- Picture in picture on phones: tap the picture-in-picture button in the player, or leave the app while a
  video plays. (On iPhone, Jace videos always can; YouTube videos go into it when iOS allows.)
- Going live from the phone apps works with the camera and microphone (Android asks the first time).
- Sharper Shorts and videos on phones: YouTube was sending a small, blurry picture to small players.
- Recommendations change every time you open the app, and videos you were just shown step aside.

## 2.1.0

- iPhone and iPad: every screen is native now, with Liquid Glass on iOS 26 (the tab bar shrinks while
  you scroll; Search is its own glass button). New native screens: comments, posts (write one with a
  picture, like, reply, open threads), notifications, library (Continue watching, Watch Later,
  playlists), history, live, your feeds, your videos, upload (pick from Photos or Files and watch it
  first), settings, and accounts.
- Android: the same native screens.
- Jace Media's own player on phones for YouTube and Jace videos: no YouTube buttons, title, or play sign.
  Double-tap a side to skip 10 seconds; speed, quality (up to 4K), captions, and full screen.
- Comments: like any comment (YouTube's, Jace's, or Jace Media's), reply to any comment, and open
  replies, on phones, the TV, and the website.
- Switch accounts, add another account, or sign one out, right in the phone apps.
- Press and hold a channel to subscribe or unsubscribe (there are no Subscribe buttons on phones).
- Home on phones: choose Home, For you, or one of your feeds.
- Going live still opens the site (it needs the camera), now full screen without the site's bars.
- Windows, Mac, Linux: the download sets itself up like Jace Launcher (choose a folder, shortcuts,
  Installed apps on Windows) and removes itself afterwards; delete it from your account menu.
  Fixed: Linux closing when a video played.

## 2.0.0

- iPhone and iPad: a native app (SwiftUI). Home, Shorts (swipe up), Subscriptions, Search, and You are
  built into the app, with Apple's player for Jace videos (picture-in-picture, AirPlay) and YouTube's
  privacy-enhanced player. Subscribe, like, dislike, Save to Watch Later, and share right from a video.
  Sign in with Jace in a secure sheet; your session is kept in the iPhone's Keychain.
- Android phones and tablets: a native app (Jetpack Compose) with the same screens, Android's own video
  player (Media3), and swipe-up Shorts. Android TV keeps the TV app.
- Posting, uploading, going live, comments, and settings open the site inside the apps, already signed in.
- Mac: the desktop app now builds for Mac too (Intel and Apple Silicon).

## 1.1.0

- TVs (LG and Android TV) open Jace Media's own TV interface: a side menu, big rows of videos, its own
  player controls, made for the remote (Back, OK, arrows, number and media buttons).
- Sign in on a TV by scanning a QR code with your phone.
- Who's watching? Pick a profile; lock any profile with a 4-number PIN. Type it with the remote's number
  buttons, use private entry (nothing on screen), or approve on your phone instead.
- The new Jace Media icon everywhere.
- New: Jace Media for Windows, Mac, and Linux, a small, fast app (a few MB) made with Tauri.

## 1.0.0

- First apps: Android phones and tablets, Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV, iPhone and iPad (AltStore or
  SideStore), and LG TVs (webOS).
- Fullscreen video, picture-in-picture (iPhone), uploads, downloads, and the back button / swipe back.
- Jace sign-in stays in the app; other websites open in your browser.
- Updates: automatic (checks when the app opens and asks to install) or manual, in Jace Media → Get the app.
- TV mode on TVs: bigger text and clear outlines for the remote.
